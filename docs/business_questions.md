# Business quetions _ Deutsh Bahn Sustainability progress analythics 
## Main Quetions
Is Deutsh Bahn actually on track to reach its public target of 80% renewable energy by 2030, based on its own historically reported progress?
## Supporting Quetions
1. How has Deutsh Bahn's renewable energy percentage changed year over year - steady growth, or sudden jumps?

2. Is Deutsche Bahn's renewable energy progress genuinely outperforming Germany's national average, or is it simply riding the same national trend as the rest of the country?
3. How has the share of women in Deutsche Bahn's leadership positions changed between 2020 and 2024?
4. What do independent third-party ESG rating agencies (CDP, EcoVadis, MSCI, ISS ESG) say about DB's sustainability performance, separate from DB's own self-reported figures?
