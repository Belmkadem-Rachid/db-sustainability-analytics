# Findings — Deutsche Bahn Sustainability Progress Analytics

## Is DB on track for its 2030 renewable energy target?

Based on Deutsche Bahn's own reported figures, the company appears to be
on track to meet its 2030 renewable energy target. Between 2019 and
2024, DB's renewable energy share grew by an average of 1.92 percentage
points per year — slightly faster than the 1.7 points per year needed
to close the remaining 10.2-point gap and reach 80% by 2030.

This is a simple linear projection based on recent average pace; actual
future progress may not be steady, as shown by the unusually large jump
in DB's renewable share between 2017 and 2018.
