# Findings — Deutsche Bahn Sustainability Progress Analytics

## Is DB on track for its 2030 renewable energy target?

Based on Deutsche Bahn's own reported figures, the company appears to be
on track to meet its 2030 renewable energy target. Between 2019 and
2024, DB's renewable energy share grew by an average of 1.92 percentage
points per year — slightly faster than the 1.7 points per year needed
to close the remaining 10.2-point gap and reach 80% by 2030.

This is a simple linear projection based on recent average pace; actual
future progress may not be steady, as shown by the unusually large jump
in DB's renewable share between 2017 and 2018.

methodology caveats were found in the sources reviewed for this metric.

## Independent ESG Ratings (Third-Party, Not DB Self-Reported)

| Agency | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CDP (climate rating) | A | A- | A |
| EcoVadis (score / tier) | 68 / Gold | 68 / Silver | 71 / Silver |
| MSCI | AA | A | A |
| ISS ESG | — | B- | — |

Two things worth noting honestly:

- **EcoVadis:** DB's numeric score held steady or improved (68 → 68 → 71),
  but its medal tier dropped from Gold to Silver. Per DB's own report,
  EcoVadis changed its tier thresholds starting 2024 (Platinum = best 1%,
  Gold = best 5%, Silver = best 15%). The tier drop likely reflects a
  change in peer benchmarking, not a decline in DB's actual performance.
- **MSCI:** rating moved from AA (2022) to A (2023–2024) — a genuine
  one-notch downgrade, worth monitoring in future years rather than
  dismissing or over-stating from a single data point.

## Summary

Across every metric checked, DB's own reported progress on renewable
energy is real, outpaces the national grid trend, and is currently on
track for its 2030 milestone. Women in leadership shows a clean, steady
improvement. Independent ESG ratings are broadly stable, with one
benchmark-driven tier change (EcoVadis) and one genuine downgrade (MSCI)
— both reported honestly rather than cherry-picked.
